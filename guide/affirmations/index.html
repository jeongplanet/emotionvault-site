Affirmation Examples and How to Write Your Own

Affirmation examples for self-esteem, money, love and anxiety, rules for writing your own and the best time to say them.

Three rules

Short, present tense, positive. Write 'I am calm' instead of 'I am not anxious.'

Self-esteem affirmations

I am enough as I am. / I treat myself with kindness. / I'm getting a little better every day. / I deserve to be loved.

Affirmations for anxiety

In this moment, I am safe. / I can do one thing at a time. / Worries pass and I remain. / One breath brings me back.

Money and work affirmations

I'm well rewarded for my work. / Opportunities come to me naturally. / I'm good with money.

When it feels like a lie

Add a process, like 'I'm becoming calmer,' or ask, 'Why am I so lucky?'
