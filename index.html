Frequently asked questions

Is Emotion Vault free?

Yes. Mood check-in, several stress relief modes, breathing and the mood log are free. Pro unlocks every mode, the full toolkit and more vision board photos. Pro passes never auto-renew.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your journal and photos are stored only on your phone, and you can back them up to a file yourself.

Does the app record my voice?

No. In the shouting mode the microphone only reads loudness to move the animation. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Is this a manifestation app or a stress relief app?

Both. Release the stress first, then write your manifestation journal and look at your vision board. A calmer mind makes the picture clearer.

Can I put my vision board on my home screen?

Yes. Add the Emotion Vault widget on iPhone or Android and your vision board photos rotate on your home screen.

Is it a replacement for therapy?

No. It's a self-care tool for everyday stress. If you are in crisis, the app shows local helplines such as 988 in the US.
