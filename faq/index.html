Using Emotion Vault

What is Emotion Vault?

It's a mind-care app where you log your mood, shake off stress with your hands, and draw the life you want with a manifestation journal and vision board. Each session goes check in, let it out, breathe, put it away.

Is Emotion Vault free?

Mood check-in, several stress relief modes, breathing and the mood log are free. Pro unlocks every mode, the full mind toolkit and more vision board photos.

Does Pro renew automatically?

No. Pro passes are one-time purchases for 1, 6 or 12 months and never auto-renew. When a pass ends you go back to the free version and keep all your entries.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. There's no sign-up or login. Install it and log today's mood right away.

Where is my journal stored?

Every entry and photo stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server, so no one else can read it.

Can I move my entries to a new phone?

Save a backup file in Settings and import it on the new phone. You can protect the file with a password.

Is it on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. It's on the App Store and Google Play with the same features.

Are there ads?

The free version may show a small banner at the bottom. Pro has no ads.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Lock it with a passcode or Face ID in Settings so no one else can open your journal.

Can I change the theme and fonts?

Choose light or dark mode, background colors, fonts including handwriting styles, and text size in Settings.

Stress relief

What's the fastest way to relieve stress?

Use your body. Tearing, tapping and a long exhale release tension quickly. Emotion Vault's let-it-out modes are built to do this in about two minutes.

Do stress relief games actually help?

The motion, sound and vibration of smashing something give built-up tension a way out. Logging how you feel afterward makes the effect last longer.

What can I do instead of breaking things?

Tear paper, hit a pillow, make a loud sound or walk fast. In the app you can smash plates and glass, crush a can or pop bubble wrap safely.

What can I do about stress at work right now?

Breathe in for 4 seconds and out for 6 for one minute at your desk. Silent bubble-wrap popping works too.

What are the signs that stress is building up?

Tight shoulders and jaw, shallow sleep, and snapping over small things. They're signals to take a break.

Does screaming relieve stress?

A loud shout deepens your breath and drains tension. The shout mode reacts to how loud you are and never records.

How long does it take to calm down?

Acute tension usually settles in 2–5 minutes. Adding a short breathing session afterward helps your mind settle too.

What daily habit helps manage stress?

Log your mood once a day and let it out briefly on hard days. Over time you'll see the patterns that wear you down.

What if I don't know why I'm stressed?

Pick a reason along with your mood each time. After a week the common causes, like work, people or sleep, start to show.

Does what works differ from person to person?

Yes. Some people need to smash, others need quiet breathing. Emotion Vault compares your mood before and after to show what works for you.

Managing anger

What should I do the moment I get angry?

Pause for ten seconds before you speak or send anything, and breathe out slowly. Then let the anger out physically so you say less you'll regret.

Is it better to hold anger in or let it out?

Bottling it up makes it explode later. Let it out somewhere safe, physically, instead of at a person.

What if I can't control my anger?

Start by noticing the moment it rises. The SOS button skips every question and takes you straight to breathing.

Why does my body heat up when I'm angry?

Your body is getting ready to fight, so your heart speeds up and muscles tense. Long exhales calm that response.

How do I deal with feeling wronged?

Name it first: 'I feel wronged.' Naming a feeling softens it and gives you room to decide what to do.

Why do I get irritated so often?

Lack of sleep, hunger and exhaustion make irritation easy. If it happens a lot, your mood log can show when.

Should I send a message while I'm angry?

Wait if you can. Written after you've calmed down, the same words hurt far less.

Can I feel worse after letting anger out?

You might feel drained or guilty afterward. Log how you feel and take a short breath to settle it.

I yelled at my child and feel guilty.

It happens to everyone. Apologize briefly once you're calm, and choose a pause signal for next time.

What if my anger is frequent and intense?

If it disrupts daily life, talk to a professional. The app is for everyday stress and doesn't replace therapy.

Anxiety & breathing

What breathing helps anxiety right away?

Breathe in for 4 seconds and out for 6 for one minute. A longer exhale tells your body it's safe.

How do I do 4-7-8 breathing?

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8. About four rounds calm your mind and make sleep easier.

What is box breathing?

Inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4, like the sides of a square. It's great before a presentation or interview.

Does the physiological sigh work?

Two short inhales followed by one long exhale releases tension fast. One or two minutes is enough.

What if anxiety hits suddenly?

Feel your feet on the floor and name five things you can see. It brings you back to the present.

How do I stop worrying?

Write the worry down and pick one thing you can do right now. Leave the rest for tomorrow.

How often should I do breathing exercises?

One to three minutes, several times a day, beats one long session. Tie it to morning, lunch and bedtime.

How do I calm nerves before a presentation?

Do two minutes of box breathing and drop your shoulders once. The SOS cards include three things to do when nervous.

What if I feel dizzy while breathing?

Breathing too deeply can cause dizziness. Slow down and return to a comfortable pace.

What if anxiety lasts a long time?

If it disrupts life for weeks, reach out to a doctor or counsellor. You don't have to handle it alone.

Sleep & insomnia

I overthink before bed. What can I do?

Write your thoughts down to get them out of your head. The bedtime wind-down helps you let the day go in three minutes.

What breathing helps me fall asleep?

4-7-8 breathing is especially good at bedtime. Lights off, lie down, four rounds.

Is using my phone before bed bad?

Bright screens and stimulating content delay sleep. Keep the last ten minutes for calm things like journaling or breathing.

Worry wakes me up at night.

Don't check the clock; do slow exhale breathing. Setting aside time to write worries during the day reduces them at night.

How do I build a bedtime routine?

Do the same short steps every night, like washing up, one line about your mood, and one minute of breathing.

What if stress keeps me awake?

Release the tension in your body first. A quick let-it-out session and some breathing make it easier to fall asleep.

Does a gratitude journal help sleep?

Recalling one good thing tilts your mind toward calm, which makes sleep easier. It doesn't need to be big.

Are naps okay?

Short naps under 20 minutes are fine. Long late-afternoon naps can push bedtime later.

Can I catch up on sleep on weekends?

An hour or two extra is fine, but sleeping in too late makes Sunday night harder.

What if insomnia goes on for weeks?

Talk to a doctor or sleep clinic if poor sleep lasts more than a few weeks.

Mood journaling

Why keep a mood journal?

Feelings become manageable once you notice and name them. Over time you see what wears you down and what restores you.

How do I write a mood journal?

Pick your mood and write one line about why. Writing daily matters more than writing a lot.

What if journaling every day is hard?

One word or one color counts. In Emotion Vault, tapping a mood face is already an entry.

What can a mood tracker app show me?

How your mood shifts by weekday, time of day and reason, and how this month compares with last.

I find it hard to name my feelings.

Use the feelings dictionary to find the closest word. The more precise the name, the faster the feeling settles.

Won't writing about negative feelings make me feel worse?

Writing a feeling down is different from dwelling on it. Most people feel lighter afterward.

Should I reread my mood journal?

Yes. A month later you can see that the hard days passed, which is comforting in itself.

How does the mood calendar work?

The Log tab colors each day by mood. Tap a day to reread what you wrote.

I'm worried someone will read my journal.

Entries stay on your phone and you can lock the app. Nothing goes to a server.

How is a mood journal different from a diary?

A diary records what happened; a mood journal records how you felt. It's a short, easy way to follow your emotional changes.

Law of attraction

What is the law of attraction?

The idea that what you often think and feel shapes your reality. You picture what you want clearly and live as if it's already yours.

How do I write a manifestation journal?

Write one line a day in the present tense as if it already happened, like 'I drink my morning coffee in a house by the sea.'

Is the law of attraction scientifically proven?

It isn't a proven scientific law. Still, regularly writing and picturing goals can sharpen focus and prompt action.

Why isn't manifesting working for me?

Often the worry that it won't happen is louder than the wish. Release built-up feelings first, then write again with a lighter mind.

How do I practice manifestation daily?

Write one line in the morning and picture the scene for one minute before bed. Streaks help you keep the habit.

Why do feelings matter in manifesting?

Wishing from anxiety amplifies lack. The key is to feel calm, as if you already have it.

How does gratitude relate to manifestation?

Gratitude for what you have shrinks the feeling of lack and makes room to receive more.

Can I manifest money?

Write a specific amount and the scene of enjoying it, then choose one step you can take today. Action matters as much as thought.

What should I be careful about when manifesting?

Clinging to outcomes breeds impatience. Choose the result and loosen your grip on how it arrives.

What's a good manifestation app?

One that combines a journal, vision board and visualization is easiest. Emotion Vault includes all three plus stress relief.

Vision boards

What is a vision board?

A board of photos and words showing the life you want. Looking at it daily keeps your goals in mind.

How do I make a vision board?

Pick photos that capture your dream home, travel, work and relationships, and add a short line to each. In Emotion Vault you can decorate them with frames and pins.

What photos should go on a vision board?

Ones that excite you the moment you see them. Choose what you truly want, not what others expect.

How many goals should a vision board have?

Three to six to start. Too many blur your focus.

Can I put my vision board on my home screen?

Yes. Add the Emotion Vault widget and your vision board photos rotate on your home screen.

How often should I look at my vision board?

At least once a day, even briefly. A widget keeps it naturally in view.

Digital or paper vision board?

Paper is fun to make by hand; digital is always with you and easy to update. On your phone, you'll see it every day.

What is a vision board video?

Emotion Vault turns your board into a short video with a guided voice, so you can practice feeling yourself inside those scenes.

Should I update my vision board when goals change?

Yes. Keep it matched to who you are now, and save achieved goals somewhere to celebrate.

Will a vision board alone make it happen?

The board sets direction. Pairing it with one small action a day brings it much closer.

Affirmations & visualization

What are affirmations?

Present-tense statements of who you want to be, like 'I'm doing enough today.'

How do I write an affirmation?

Keep it short, present tense and positive. Write 'I am calm' instead of 'I am not anxious.'

What if affirmations feel like lies?

Add a process, like 'I'm becoming calmer,' or turn it into a question: 'Why am I so lucky?'

When is the best time for affirmations?

Right after waking and just before sleep, when your mind is most open.

How do I visualize?

Close your eyes and picture the day your wish comes true, including sounds, smells and feelings. One minute is enough.

What is the 1-minute visualization?

A guided feature in Emotion Vault where a calm voice walks you through your wished-for scene for one minute. Choose a male or female voice.

What if I can't visualize clearly?

Look at a photo first, then close your eyes. Or start with the feeling rather than the image.

Do I need to say affirmations out loud?

Saying them aloud feels stronger, but reading or writing them works too. Use whatever feels natural.

What is the state map?

An Emotion Vault feature for mapping the qualities you want to grow into, which you can turn into daily affirmations.

Can I combine affirmations with a gratitude journal?

Yes. Gratitude fills the present and affirmations shape the future, so they reinforce each other.

Letters to your future self

How do I write a letter to my future self?

Open the 'To my future self' card on the home screen, write what you want to say, and choose the date and time it arrives. It shows up with a notification.

Why write a letter to your future self?

It helps you see today's worries from a distance and later shows how far you've come. Writing the future you want also nudges your mind toward it.

How far ahead should I send a future letter?

One month is an easy start. Birthdays, New Year, or big days like exams or a new job make it even more meaningful.

Can I cancel or read a letter early?

No. A letter sent to the future stays sealed until its day, which makes receiving it feel special.

What should I write in a future letter?

Your days lately, what you're worried about, and who you hope to be. Starting with 'I wonder what kind of day you're having' makes it easy.

The subconscious mind

What is the subconscious mind?

The deeper layer of your mind that guides thoughts and actions without you noticing. It's shaped by repeated words, feelings and habits.

Can I change my subconscious beliefs?

Repetition is key. Reading affirmations daily, briefly picturing the life you want and releasing built-up feelings all help.

Why say affirmations before sleep?

Right before sleep your thoughts slow and your mind relaxes, so words sink in more easily. Try the 1-minute visualization and bedtime wind-down together.

How do I deal with negative feelings stored in my subconscious?

Name the feeling and let it out physically. Suppressed feelings don't disappear; they pile up until you notice and release them.

How are the subconscious and manifestation connected?

If you wish for something but secretly believe it won't happen, your actions won't follow. Manifesting gains power when the scene you want feels natural deep inside.

Self-care & self-esteem

Where do I start with self-care?

Sleep, meals and short breaks first. Then say one kind thing to yourself today.

What can I do when my self-esteem is low?

Finish one tiny task and acknowledge it: 'I did it.' Small wins change how you see yourself.

I'm too hard on myself.

Imagine what you'd say to a friend in the same spot, then say it to yourself. That's where self-love begins.

I feel behind when I compare myself to others.

Everyone has their own pace and timing. Compare yourself with yesterday's you, not with others.

Other people's opinions shake me.

Their view is their lens, not the whole of you. What you control is your response.

I think I'm burned out.

Drop one task you don't strictly need and simply rest in that time. If it persists, seriously consider rest and counselling.

How do I cope with a breakup or loss?

Grief doesn't need to hurry. The letting-go cards walk through that time slowly with you.

What is the 7-day mind course?

A short course with one step a day, from naming feelings to building self-care habits.

Isn't self-care selfish?

No. You care for others better when you're okay. Looking after yourself is responsible.

What if it's too much to handle alone?

You don't have to. In the US call or text 988, in the UK call Samaritans at 116 123, or contact your local crisis line.
